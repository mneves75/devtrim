# 2026-10-04

## TUI: `A` "does nothing", and what PREVIEW means

The owner reported that `A` (select all) did nothing and asked why `PREVIEW`
shows beside the menu items. A real PTY session against a disposable home
showed three things:

- In "Scan everything", which is read-only, Space, `A` and `s` were ignored
  silently, and `a` said "This result has no actionable findings" beside
  "8 KB actionable". That is where the owner pressed `A`.
- In a category view `A` works, but it toggles: everything starts selected, so
  the first press leaves everything out while the footer said "A all".
- The menu badges (READ-ONLY, PREVIEW, PERMANENT) were never explained.

Fix (tests first, red before the change): the scan keeps `ScanResult.sections`,
so a key that cannot act names the highlighted finding's own category view
("b, then 2 (caches)") only when that view can apply it; every other inert key
says why; the footer names `A`'s next effect; the menu pane explains the badge.
Every footer line fits at 64×18 (the new "A none" made one line 63 columns, so
Space became "Space pick"). Rejected alternative: a key that jumps from a scan
finding to its category view; it adds a key the 64×18 help overlay must hold,
for a path the status line already names.

Also on 2026-10-03/04: Codex-release tests take an injected `lsof` probe
(193b844, 2b1a43c); the live probe refuses about once in 200 runs under load
with "lsof reported a mapped path that is not absolute", which a real scan can
show as a Codex-release error. Dependabot PRs #9 and #12 were closed as
superseded with the owner's approval.

The version is 0.10.7 in source; nothing is tagged. The demo videos
(`video/src/DevtrimDemo.tsx`, `media/brag`) still show the 0.10.6 menu and
footer text; they are illustrations and were not re-rendered.
