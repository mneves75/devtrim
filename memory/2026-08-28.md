# 2026-08-28

## CODING_STANDARDS.md rewritten, five invariants moved into gates

`CODING_STANDARDS.md` was two untracked lines feeding the `/code-review`
skill's Standards axis. Read the skill's source: standards discovery is one
fuzzy sentence that never names `AGENTS.md`/`CLAUDE.md`, citation is mandatory
(`file + the rule`), and the sub-agent is told to skip anything tooling
enforces. That dictated the shape — addressable `S<n>` rules, an explicit
"already enforced, do not report" table, and an "inherited invariants, cite do
not restate" table pointing at `SECURITY.md § Defense layers` and
`CLAUDE.md § Conventions` instead of duplicating them a fourth time.

Audit of `src/` found several invariants held at 100% with no gate behind them.
Those became gates, not prose:

- `Cargo.toml`: `unsafe_code = "forbid"` plus clippy `unwrap_used`,
  `expect_used`, `panic`, `unreachable`, `todo`, `unimplemented`, `dbg_macro`,
  `allow_attributes_without_reason`. All passed on the first run — production
  was already at zero. Cost: a `cfg_attr(test, allow(..., reason))` in
  `src/lib.rs`, a file-level allow in `tests/cli.rs`, and one reasoned allow on
  the `unreachable!` arm in `src/app.rs`.
- `rules/no-shell-invocation.yml` — the `CLAUDE.md` "never add shell-string
  execution" convention had zero automated protection until now.
- `rules/no-abbreviated-bindings.yml` — `|e|`/`Err(e)`/`let val` against a
  denylist, in binding positions only.

### Lessons

- An `#[allow]` on a macro *invocation* is silently ignored (`unused_attribute`
  fires). It has to go on the enclosing match arm.
- ast-grep `inside:` defaults to the immediate parent — `stopBy: end` is
  required or `|mut err|` and `Err(ref e)` slip through. And `stopBy: end`
  without `field: pattern` walks into parameter *types*, so
  `std::num::NonZeroU32` matched the `num` denylist entry. Both found by
  planting the violation in a scratch tree, not by reading the rule.
- Line numbers in a doc no gate checks are change detectors. Every precedent in
  `CODING_STANDARDS.md` cites file + a searchable phrase instead.
- autoreview P3 raised five findings across two passes; two were real (both my
  own new rules had false negatives), one was wrong (claimed CI never scans —
  `ast-grep scan` is the next line in the same step, outside the diff hunk), and
  one proposed a fix that would have misfired on `src/cli.rs`, where `"bash"`,
  `"zsh"`, `"fish"` are legitimately shell *names* for `completions`. Checking
  before applying was the whole value of the pass.
- The shell lint's residual gap (a shell reached through a variable) is closed
  by prose, not by a fragile structural allowlist — that is standard S12.
